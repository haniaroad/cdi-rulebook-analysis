The important model boundaries

WorkflowContent currently authors schema version 6, while readers admit earlier persisted versions. Its roots include terminology, workflowStates, workflowTransitions, optional activityGroups, journey, routingRules and ruleDefinitions.

A workflow predicate is a single AND or OR group of clauses. Operators are is, is_not, gt, gte, lt, lte, is_set and is_not_set. Sources include input, system, activity_outcome, linked_record, library_value and spatial. This grammar does not support nested groups like RMS rule trees.
